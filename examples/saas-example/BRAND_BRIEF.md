# Brand Brief — Synapse Analytics

## Core Identity
- **Company**: Synapse Analytics
- **What We Do**: Customer journey analytics platform that connects siloed data to reveal the full path from first touch to conversion
- **Audience**: Marketing operations teams, growth marketers, and analytics engineers at mid-market B2B companies (50-500 employees)
- **Value Proposition**: See your entire customer journey in one place — no SQL required
- **Tagline**: Every touchpoint. One picture.

## Brand Personality
- **Adjectives**: Intelligent, Clear, Confident, Modern, Empowering
- **Tone**: Technically credible but accessible — like a smart colleague, not a sales pitch
- **Emotional Target**: "This actually shows me what I need to know" — clarity and relief from data chaos
- **Voice**: Direct and data-driven. Lead with outcomes. No buzzwords. Examples over abstractions.

## Visual Direction
- **Color Mood**: Deep indigo/violet for intelligence and depth, with amber/orange accents for energy and action. Light mode default.
- **Preferred Colors**: Indigo primary (#3730A3), amber accent (#F59E0B), clean white background
- **Typography Feel**: Modern geometric sans — clean, tech-forward, readable at small sizes
- **Layout Density**: Information-rich but organized — dashboards, tables, feature grids
- **Imagery Style**: Abstract data visualizations, clean UI screenshots, no stock photos of people

## Content Strategy
- **Pages Needed**: Home, Features, Pricing, About, Contact
- **Primary CTA**: Start Free Trial (no credit card)
- **Hero Message**: Every touchpoint. One picture. — See your full customer journey without writing a single query.

## Differentiation
- **Competitors**: Mixpanel (event-focused, not journey), Amplitude (complex, expensive), GA4 (shallow attribution)
- **How We're Different**: Journey-first (not event-first), no SQL requirement, connects any data source in <1 hour

## Brand Guidelines
- Always lead with the outcome, not the feature: "See attribution in minutes" not "Multi-touch attribution module"
- Use concrete numbers: "connects in <1 hour", "14-day free trial", "no credit card required"
- Technical credibility: mention integrations (Segment, Snowflake, dbt), don't hide complexity
- CTAs should reduce friction: "Start Free Trial" > "Request a Demo" > "Contact Sales"
