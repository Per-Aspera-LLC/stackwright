# Brand Brief — Meridian Law Group

## Core Identity
- **Company**: Meridian Law Group
- **What We Do**: Full-service law firm serving businesses and families in the greater Portland area since 1987
- **Audience**: Local business owners (primary), families seeking estate planning (secondary), personal injury clients (tertiary)
- **Value Proposition**: 35+ years of trusted legal expertise with the personal attention of a boutique firm
- **Tagline**: Trusted Counsel. Proven Results.

## Brand Personality
- **Adjectives**: Authoritative, Trustworthy, Approachable, Established, Precise
- **Tone**: Professional but warm — serious about outcomes, not intimidating
- **Emotional Target**: Confidence and reassurance — "I'm in good hands, they've seen this before"
- **Voice**: Clear, confident, jargon-free. "We handle your legal needs" not "We leverage synergistic legal frameworks"

## Visual Direction
- **Color Mood**: Deep navy and gold — authority and trust. Not cold/corporate blue but warm, established law firm.
- **Preferred Colors**: Navy primary (#1A2C4E), gold accent (#C9A84C), warm cream background (#FAFAF8)
- **Typography Feel**: Classic serif headings (authority), clean sans body (readability)
- **Layout Density**: Balanced — not overly dense, not minimal. Information-rich where needed (practice areas)
- **Imagery Style**: Professional photography (attorneys, office) — we'll reference placeholder paths

## Content Strategy
- **Pages Needed**: Home, About, Practice Areas, Contact
- **Primary CTA**: Schedule a Consultation (phone + form)
- **Hero Message**: 35 years of trusted legal counsel — protecting what matters most

## Differentiation
- **Competitors**: Big downtown firms (impersonal), solo practitioners (limited capacity)
- **How We're Different**: Boutique personal attention with established firm resources. Real attorneys answer the phone.

## Brand Guidelines
- Use "Consultation" not "Free Consultation" — respect client's time
- Never use legal jargon without explanation
- Warmth comes from specificity: "we've helped over 2,000 Portland families" not "we help many clients"
- Gold accent sparingly — CTAs, highlights, awards
